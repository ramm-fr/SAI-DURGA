Our Studio & Philosophy

A family-run clay idol workshop rooted in tradition, located in Palavalasa, Sompeta, Srikakulam.

Our Story

Sai Durga Arts is a family workshop in Palavalasa village, Sompeta mandal, Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh. For generations, our family has been sculpting Ganesh and Durga idols from the natural clay found along our local riverbeds.

While the market shifted to cheap Plaster of Paris idols that pollute our waters, we stayed committed to the traditional method. Every idol we produce is 100% natural clay — no chemicals, no synthetic binders, no toxic paints.

Our mission is simple: provide beautiful, devotional idols that are kind to the environment. After pooja, our idols dissolve fully in water within an hour, returning harmlessly to the earth.

Today we serve customers across Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh, and ship pan-India for larger orders. From a 3-inch home idol to a 5-foot mandapam piece — everything is hand-made with care.

Process

How We Make Each Idol

01

Clay Collection

We source pure natural clay from riverbeds and agricultural land around Palavalasa. The soil is cleaned, sifted, and prepared into workable clay by mixing with water and kneading by hand.

02

Hand Sculpting

Each idol is sculpted entirely by hand — no molds, no machines. Our artisans use traditional tools and techniques passed down through generations. Every piece is unique.

03

Sun Drying

Finished sculptures are dried naturally under sunlight for 5-7 days depending on size. No kilns or ovens. This preserves the clay's ability to dissolve easily in water.

04

Natural Painting

For painted idols, we use only botanical pigments — turmeric, kumkum, natural earth oxides. No synthetic paints, no chemical dyes. Colors are safe for water immersion.

05

Packing & Delivery

Each idol is wrapped in soft cloth, cushioned with straw, and placed in a wooden or cardboard crate. We ensure damage-free delivery to your doorstep.
